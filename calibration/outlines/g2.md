KEY QUESTION (level 0): How can PEIP achieve positive operating cash flow within the next 12 months?

1. How to increase cash inflow?
   1.1 How to optimize revenue from existing operations?
       - Increasing price on existing products?
           - Which segment should we focus on?
           - How much should we increase the price by?
       - Increasing quantity of customers?
           - Which segment should we focus on?
           - How should we attract more customers within each existing segment?
   1.2 How to pursue new growth opportunities?
       - How can we further diversify product portfolio?
           - Should we adapt current products for new markets?
           - Should we launch new product lines for off-seasons?
       - How to expand into new distribution channels?
           - Should we expand locally?
           - Should we expand overseas?
2. How to decrease cash outflow?
   2.1 How to reduce OPEX?
       - Rent
       - Utilities
       - Administration
       - Marketing
   2.2 How to reduce COGS?
       - How to reduce COGS for traditional products?
       - How to reduce COGS for organic products?
3. How to improve cash cycle?
   3.1 How to optimize working capital?
       - Payables
       - Receivables
       - Inventory turnover
   3.2 How to bridge the gap of cash inflow and outflow?
       - How to reduce the frequency of cash outflow?
       - How to increase the frequency of cash inflow?
4. How to increase cash control?
   4.1 How to optimize financial control?
       - How to increase accounting accuracy?
       - How to increase accounting stability?
   4.2 How to optimize organizational control?
       - How to improve the role of the CEO?
           - Reduce dependence on CEO
           - Prioritize CEO tasks strategically
       - How to improve the role of the workforce?
           - How to hire more staff?
           - How to retain current staff for longer?
