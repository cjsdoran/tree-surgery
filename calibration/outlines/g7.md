KEY QUESTION (level 0): How can PEIP achieve sustainable profitability and long-term growth?

1. How can PEIP maximise gross margin?
   1.1 How can PEIP optimize its 80+ item product portfolio?
       - How can PEIP address its low-margin, low-volume SKUs to improve profitability?
           - Cut products with low margins and focus on bestsellers.
       - How can PEIP determine the optimal number of SKUs to maximize total profit?
           - Test and see what number of SKUs maximizes total profit.
   1.2 How can PEIP prioritize high-margin distribution channels?
       - How can PEIP capture high-end external wholesale distributors?
           - Focus on high-end specialty importers
       - How can PEIP fulfill the Japanese export demand for the organic line?
           - Run winter production for organic line and use spare capacity
       - How can PEIP convert retail visitors into repeat mail-order customers?
           - Collect emails in store. Offer free shipping on first online order.
2. How can PEIP stabilize its capital structure and cash flow?
   2.1 How can PEIP release tied-up working capital?
       - How can PEIP rapidly reduce excess inventory?
           - Offer exclusive bundles in off season
   2.2 How can PEIP restore lender confidence to secure working capital financing?
       - How can PEIP resolve the instability in its bookkeeping and financial reporting?
           - Employ external audit or advisor
3. How can PEIP optimize its operational structure?
   3.1 How can PEIP restructure management to focus on high-value growth?
       - How can PEIP reallocate Bruce's duties away from daily operations?
           - Align his KPIs with the core businesses
       - How should PEIP structure a new sales manager role to maximize channel effectiveness?
           - Commission-based role focusing on high-margin channels
   3.2 How can PEIP rationalize non-core business units?
       - How can PEIP eliminate the financial drag of Garden?
           - Transfer management to the non-profits
           - Outsource operations to volunteers
       - How can PEIP simplify restaurant operations?
           - Shrink menu and standardize seasonal staff
   3.3 How can PEIP improve production efficiency
       - How to minimize turnover costs and retraining expenses?
           - Offer bonus for workers coming back the next season
           - Cross training 5 top performers as year round staff
       - How to eliminate the net loss incurred on shipping distribution?
           - Raise the threshold of free shipping
           - Implement surcharge for US orders
